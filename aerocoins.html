How it works

Earned. Never bought.

AeroCoins do not change moderation, voting power, rankings or XP. They exist to reward contribution and unlock community cosmetics in later V10 phases.

01 / REFERRALS

5 + 5 AeroCoins.

Invite a new photographer with your unique link. When they reach five approved aviation photographs, both accounts earn five AeroCoins. Pressing Share alone never pays coins.

02 / FLIGHTLINE STREAK

Two approved photos per day.

Qualify a day with two approved submissions. Rewards grow with the streak: 1 AC, 2 AC from day 3, 3 AC from day 7, 4 AC from day 14 and 5 AC from day 30 onward.

03 / FAIR MODERATION

Approval delays don't hurt you.

If your photographs are approved later, the streak is credited to the day you originally submitted them. Rejected photographs never count.

Permanent early-community status

Founding 100.

The first 100 photographers to reach 20 approved aviation photographs receive a permanent numbered badge. It cannot be bought and it cannot be earned after all 100 places are claimed.

The economy stays fair

XP is not AeroCoins.

XP remains permanent progression. AeroCoins are spendable credits. No AeroCoin purchase can buy likes, XP, moderation priority, ranking boosts or voting power.

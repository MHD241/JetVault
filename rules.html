Scottish.aero / Archive standards

GOOD FRAMES.
CLEAR RULES.

These standards apply to community submissions. They keep the global archive consistent, useful and genuinely photographic.

01

Ownership & permission

Only submit photographs you took yourself or have explicit permission to submit. Do not upload images copied from social media, airline pages, image searches, friends, other photographers or paid libraries unless you own the necessary rights.

Reject when

Ownership is doubtful, a visible third-party copyright mark is present, or the uploader cannot reasonably claim the work.

02

Authenticity & AI

Scottish.aero is a photography archive. Fully AI-generated aircraft images, synthetic “photographs”, fake liveries presented as real, and major generative replacements are not accepted.

Normal photography editing is fine: exposure, colour, crop, noise reduction, sharpening, lens corrections and sensible object cleanup. Editing must not materially fake the aircraft, operator, airport, weather or event.

AI detectors are supporting evidence only.

Detection tools can be wrong. A detector score by itself should never be the only reason a moderator rejects a photograph.

03

Aviation subject & composition

The aviation subject should be obvious and intentional: aircraft, helicopters, airport operations or another clear aviation scene. The aircraft does not always need to fill the frame, but the photograph should have a reason to exist in an aviation archive.

Usually acceptable

Intentional close crops, silhouettes, atmospheric shots, panning shots and airport scenes when the result is clearly deliberate.

04

Technical quality

The image should be reasonably sharp where it matters, properly exposed enough to read the subject, and free of extreme compression, accidental blur, destructive noise reduction or obvious processing damage.

We do not require perfect professional equipment. A strong phone photograph can pass; an expensive-camera photograph can fail. Judge the frame, not the gear.

05

Watermarks, borders & edits

Small, discreet photographer watermarks are acceptable. Large logos, giant names, timestamps, social handles across the aircraft, decorative frames or text that distracts from the photograph may be rejected.

Heavy filters, fake motion blur, fake skies, generated aircraft details and edits that turn the image into artwork rather than a photograph do not belong in the main archive.

06

Metadata should be honest

Aircraft model and airline/operator should be accurate. Registration, airport and date should be supplied when known. “Unknown” is better than inventing an answer.

If a moderator spots an obvious metadata problem, reject the submission with a clear note explaining what needs correcting.

07

Duplicates & burst sequences

Do not flood the archive with near-identical frames from the same burst. Submit the strongest photograph. A second image of the same aircraft is fine when the angle, moment, light or context is meaningfully different.

08

Safety, privacy & legality

Do not encourage trespassing, unsafe behaviour or access to restricted areas. Photographs should comply with applicable local rules. Avoid publishing sensitive personal information that is unrelated to the aviation subject.

Immediate rejection

Content that clearly promotes dangerous airport behaviour, illegal access, harassment or non-consensual private information.

For Scottish.aero Crew

The approval checklist.

Every community photo should pass the same eight checks before an Approve button is used in Creator Studio.

01Ownership / permission looks valid
02No obvious AI-generated or synthetic image
03Aviation subject is clear and intentional
04Technical quality is archive-worthy
05No obstructive watermark or destructive edit
06Metadata has been checked
07Not an unnecessary near-duplicate
08No obvious safety, privacy or legal issue
APPROVE

All eight pass.

The photograph enters the public archive and the photographer receives an approval notification.

REJECT + NOTE

Tell them what failed.

Choose the closest reason and leave a useful note. “Bad photo” is not a moderation note.

UNSURE

Do not guess.

Leave it pending and ask another crew moderator. Uncertainty is better than a careless rejection.

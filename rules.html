Community rules

Keep the archive useful.

1. Upload your own work.

Only submit aviation photographs you have the right to publish.

2. Metadata should be truthful.

Registration, aircraft, operator, airport and date should be as accurate as possible.

3. No spam or abusive content.

Comments, profiles and captions must stay relevant and respectful.

4. Moderation is final.

Crew may reject duplicates, unusable files, incorrect submissions or content that does not fit the archive.

5. Credit photographers.

Do not remove or misrepresent creator attribution.

6. Aviation safety matters.

Do not encourage trespass, unsafe spotting or interference with airport operations.

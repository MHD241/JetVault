The build

One aviation idea.
A whole platform.

The goal was not to drop photographs into a template. Scottish.aero was built as its own product, with an identity and systems made specifically for an aviation community.

01

Visual identity

Cinematic dark aviation styling, responsive layouts, HUD details, motion and a consistent interface across the platform.

02

Community system

Public accounts, photographer profiles, follows, likes, comments, bookmarks, notifications and community submissions.

03

Creator Studio

Crew publishing, photo management, moderation, reports, bug tracking and the tools needed to operate the archive.

04

Games Lab

Aviation games and interactive experiences designed to make the site something people can use, not only scroll through.

05

Responsive build

Desktop, tablet and mobile behaviour with dedicated mobile navigation and touch-first interactions.

06

Backend

Authentication, permissions, storage, database policies and moderation workflows powered by Supabase.

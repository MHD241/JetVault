The rules

Fair for everyone.

Four rules keep early access honest, so the archive stays full of real photographers and real photos.

  • One link, one partner

    Each link works for one person. Nobody can be in two pairs, and you cannot pair with yourself.

  • Your own 75

    Photos are never shared or moved between accounts. Each of you needs 75 of your own. If your partner has none, you are not unlocked.

  • Approved photos only

    Only photos that have passed review count towards your number.

  • Locked once paired

    You can cancel your link until someone uses it. After that, the pair stays as it is.

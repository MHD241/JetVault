Why Scottish.aero exists

About.

An aviation photography community born in Scotland — built around proper photographer credit, useful aircraft data and the people who keep looking up.

The idea

Not a feed. An archive.

Scottish.aero began as a surprise home for a small aviation group in Scotland. The original crew — Mohammed Shnina, Ellis Martin and Arran Gordon — remain permanently badged as Scottish.aero Crew.

V7 opens the archive to aviation photographers worldwide. Community members can create profiles, follow each other, discuss photographs and submit their own work for moderation before it appears publicly.

The goal is still the same: photography first, proper credit always, and enough aircraft detail to make the archive genuinely useful to aviation people.

Photographer first

Ownership follows the authenticated uploader, not a manually typed credit.

Details matter

Registrations, aircraft, operators, airports, dates and captions can all be edited later.

Made to grow

GitHub Pages serves the experience while Supabase handles accounts, moderation, social activity and community submissions.

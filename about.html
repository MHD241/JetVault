About Scottish.aero

Made by people who look up.

Scottish.aero is an aviation photography group built around Scotland’s airports, aircraft and the community that follows them.

The idea

A Scottish home for aviation photography.

Not a stock gallery. Not an airline database. A place for the images, movements and little moments that aviation enthusiasts actually care about.

01

Photography first.

The aircraft is the subject. The atmosphere, weather and photographer make the frame.

02

Built around Scotland.

Edinburgh, Glasgow, Prestwick, Aberdeen, Inverness, Dundee and wherever else something worth seeing turns up.

What matters
01Credit every photographer.
02Keep aircraft information useful, not cluttered.
03Let the images do most of the talking.
04Make the community feel like it owns the place.

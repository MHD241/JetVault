Why Scottish.aero exists

About.

A home for an aviation group in Scotland to showcase the photographs they make together — and give every image the detail and credit it deserves.

The idea

Not a feed. An archive.

Scottish.aero is built around aviation photography and the people behind it.

Each crew account has its own identity. When Mohammed, Ellis or Arran uploads a photograph, the website records that photographer automatically. Aircraft details can be refined afterwards without ever losing the original credit.

The first live collection is seven photographs by Arran Gordon. Registration fields are deliberately marked Unknown so the crew can complete the data from the private dashboard.

Photographer first

Ownership follows the authenticated uploader, not a manually typed credit.

Details matter

Registrations, aircraft, operators, airports, dates and captions can all be edited later.

Made to grow

GitHub Pages serves the site while Supabase handles private accounts, uploads and visit data.
